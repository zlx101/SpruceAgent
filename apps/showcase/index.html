Safety model

Power is useful only when the boundary is visible.

  • Local-first runtime and bearer-token gateway.
  • LLM output becomes draft plans, not direct action.
  • Risky actions require approval tickets.
  • Imported skill packages become candidates, not approved skills.
  • Runs, workflows, and skills produce inspectable traces.
