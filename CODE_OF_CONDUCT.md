# Code of Conduct

SpruceAgent is built for serious technical collaboration across languages, regions, and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, factual, and respectful.
- Critique code, design, tests, and claims rather than people.
- Prefer evidence, reproducible steps, and concrete tradeoffs.
- Respect user privacy, local context, and security-sensitive information.
- Make room for contributors working in English, Chinese, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic, or other languages.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information, secrets, logs, or user data without consent.
- Misrepresenting project capabilities, benchmarks, safety properties, or affiliations.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass safety review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent blocking from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to the repository maintainers through GitHub. For security-sensitive matters, follow `SECURITY.md` instead.
